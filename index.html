About me

I'm a data scientist in Rome, working in the Innovation team at doValue. I build credit risk models for debt collection: probability of default models, and propensity-to-pay models that help decide which debtors to contact first. Most of my work is in Python, PySpark and SQL on Databricks.

I started in quantitative finance: a bachelor's in management and economics at Bocconi, a master's in finance at Nova in Lisbon focused on financial markets and econometrics, then a year as a quantitative trading risk analyst at BBVA in Madrid. I moved into data science with a master's at Sapienza, and spent two years as a data scientist at Poste Italiane, detecting fraud on the national digital identity system and forecasting cyber attacks with deep learning, before joining doValue in 2025.

I care about models that hold up outside the notebook: validated on data they haven't seen, calibrated so the numbers mean something, and monitored once they're in use. My side projects follow the same idea, like a loan default model I took all the way to a live API.

I work in Italian, English and Spanish, and get by in Portuguese.

What I'm working on

  • A propensity-to-pay classifier at doValue that scores newly onboarded debtors so agents can prioritise outreach. It is currently in development.
  • A shared dev, test and prod workflow on Databricks for my team, with code under version control.
  • Public portfolio work on taking models to production. The latest is a loan default scoring service deployed on Azure.
